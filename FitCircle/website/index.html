What Makes FitCircle Special

Everything your family needs
to stay active together

From step tracking to family challenges — FitCircle keeps everyone moving and motivated.

👟

Step Tracking

Real-time Android step tracking. Your steps sync automatically to FitCircle. Manual entry available as a fallback — your progress is never lost.

🏆

Family Leaderboard

See where every family member stands right now. Daily, weekly, monthly and all-time rankings. The leaderboard updates in real time.

⚡

Overtake System

The signature FitCircle feature. Always know the exact number of steps needed to overtake the person above you. Get a celebration when you do.

💪

Exercise Logging

Log 12 exercise types — running, cycling, push-ups, yoga and more. Track duration, reps, sets and distance. Review your complete history.

🎯

Family Challenges

Combined step goals, monthly challenges, most-exercise competitions. Work together or compete — FitCircle supports both.

🔥

Daily Streaks

Build momentum with daily goal streaks. FitCircle tracks your current streak, your best streak and your completed days this month.

🎖️

Achievements

Unlock milestones like 10K Club, 7 Day Warrior, Overtaker and Family Champion. Nine achievements to collect — all earned through real activity.

📡

Real-Time Updates

Powered by Supabase Realtime. When a family member walks, everyone's leaderboard updates automatically. No manual refresh required.

Getting Started

From download to family leaderboard
in minutes

1

Download the APK

Click the Download APK button. The file downloads directly to your Android device from GitHub Releases.

2

Install FitCircle

Open the downloaded file. If Android asks to allow installs from this source, enable it. Then tap Install.

3

Create Your Account

Sign up with your email address. Set your name, profile picture and your personal daily step goal.

4

Create or Join a Circle

Create a family circle and share your unique code (like FIT-7K92X). Or enter a code to join an existing family.

5

Allow Step Tracking

Grant the activity permission so FitCircle can track your steps automatically throughout the day.

6

Start Walking!

Your steps sync to the family leaderboard. Walk more to climb the ranks. FitCircle tells you exactly how close you are to overtaking the person above you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Yes — FitCircle is completely free to download and use. There are no subscriptions, no hidden fees, and no premium tiers in V1.

FitCircle requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later. This covers the vast majority of modern Android devices. iOS support is planned for a future release.

FitCircle looks at your position on the family leaderboard and finds the person directly above you. It then calculates the exact number of extra steps you need to walk to overtake them — down to the single step. This number updates in real time as both you and your family member walk.

When you create a family circle, FitCircle generates a unique family code like FIT-7K92X. Share this code with your family members. They enter the code in the app and instantly join your circle. You can see everyone's steps and ranking on the shared leaderboard.

Yes. FitCircle uses the Android step counter sensor to track your steps automatically in the background. If your device doesn't support automatic tracking or you deny the permission, you can manually add steps any time.

Your fitness data is only visible to members of your family circle. FitCircle uses Supabase Row Level Security to enforce this at the database level — it is not just frontend filtering. Workout notes are always private. No data is shared publicly. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

FitCircle is open source. The complete source code — Flutter app, website, Supabase SQL and all documentation — is available on GitHub ↗.
