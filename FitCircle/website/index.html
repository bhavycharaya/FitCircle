What Makes FitCircle Special

Everything your family needs
to stay active together

From step tracking to family challenges — FitCircle keeps everyone moving and motivated.

👟

Step Tracking

Real-time Android step tracking. Your steps sync automatically to FitCircle. Manual entry available as a fallback — your progress is never lost.

🏆

Family Leaderboard

See where every family member stands right now. Daily, weekly, monthly and all-time rankings. The leaderboard updates in real time.

⚡

Overtake System

The signature FitCircle feature. Always know the exact number of steps needed to overtake the person above you.

💪

Exercise Logging

Log 12 exercise types — running, cycling, push-ups, yoga and more. Track duration, reps, sets and distance.

🎯

Family Challenges

Combined step goals, monthly challenges, most-exercise competitions. Work together or compete.

🔥

Daily Streaks

Build momentum with daily goal streaks. FitCircle tracks your current streak and best streak.

🎖️

Achievements

Unlock milestones like 10K Club, 7 Day Warrior, Overtaker and Family Champion.

📡

Real-Time Updates

Powered by Supabase Realtime. Family leaderboard updates automatically.

Getting Started

From download to family leaderboard
in minutes

1

Download the APK

Click Download APK. The file downloads directly from GitHub Releases.

2

Install FitCircle

Open the downloaded file and tap Install.

3

Create Your Account

Sign up with your email address and set your profile.

4

Create or Join a Circle

Create a family circle or join one using its unique code.

5

Allow Step Tracking

Grant activity permission so FitCircle can track steps automatically.

6

Start Walking!

Your steps sync to the family leaderboard. Walk more and climb the ranks.
